# Scoring Scale

Bulk reference for `/feature-request-analysis`. Read at Step 2, before scoring any cluster.

## The dimensions

**The anchors are fixed, not relative to this batch** (`references/protocols/tracker-writes.md` §1). A 5 has to mean the same thing in a thin month and a heavy one, or the score cannot rank a cluster from today against one from last quarter. Replace the example anchors below with counts and conditions true of your own book, write them down beside the scale, and treat a change to one as a re-score of everything already scored.

| Dimension | 5 means | 1 means | What to measure |
|-----------|---------|---------|-----------------|
| **Frequency** | Asked by 20+ distinct accounts | One account, once | How often does this come up? |
| **Intensity** | Named in a churn or non-renewal conversation | Mentioned as a nice-to-have | How badly does its absence hurt? |
| **Strategic fit** | Directly moves a current Key Result | No line to any current objective | Does this move a KR? |
| **Breadth** | Affects every user type | One narrow segment | Niche or universal? |

**Weighted score:** (Frequency × 2 + Intensity × 2 + Strategic fit × 3 + Breadth × 1) / 8

**Four dimensions, and all four are in the formula.** Scope clarity was scored here and then left out of the weighted score, which made it a question everyone answered and nothing consumed. It is a readiness question, not a value one — ask it in Step 4, where the feasibility filter already lives.

**An unassessed dimension stays empty and drops out of the denominator** — it does not score 1 and it does not score 0. Empty means nobody looked; 1 means it is genuinely weak, and the ranking should keep those apart (`references/protocols/tracker-writes.md` §4).

Flag anything scoring above 4.0 as P0 consideration.

Where the tracker can hold these, **write the four factors into their own fields and let it compute the score.** Never type the computed value: it goes stale the moment a factor moves, and it goes stale without telling anyone.
